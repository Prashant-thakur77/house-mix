# Narrator voice references (licence record)

- `male.wav`: the first 17.5 s of `indian_voice.mpeg`, a recording supplied by the project owner for his films'
  narration (high-pass, light denoise, 24 kHz mono, -20 LUFS).
- `female.wav`: three sentences from the LJ Speech dataset, which is in the public domain; joined with 0.3 s gaps,
  high-passed, 24 kHz mono, -20 LUFS.
- `narrator.wav`: the TTS model's own built-in voice (kept for reference; not used by the current cut).

The narration is synthesised from these references with an MIT-licensed TTS model. No other person's voice is used.
