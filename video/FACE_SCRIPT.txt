House Mix film: what you say to camera
======================================

How to record
- Laptop or phone camera, landscape (horizontal), lens at eye level.
- Light in front of you (window or lamp behind the camera), quiet room, plain wall.
- Look into the lens, not at the screen.
- Leave 2 seconds of silence before and after each line.
- Say each line twice (two takes). This film is energetic: stand if you can, smile on
  the first line, lean in on "the part sponsors care about", and hit the last words of
  each line clearly. Fast is fine; rushed is not.
- Say the number first, softly ("line one"), so I can find it.
- One file is fine for everything. Save it in ~/Downloads with "housemix-face" in the
  name (housemix-face.mp4 or .mov) and tell me when it is there.

The lines

1.  I'm Prashant. I build for the people who run the room.

2.  They plan every night for a crowd they've never met.

3.  So I built something that asks Qloo who's really coming.

4.  This is House Mix.
    (Say it like a reveal. Short pause before "House Mix".)

5.  Now, the part sponsors care about.

6.  If you're the brand paying for tonight, here's how you know you belong.

7.  We tested it the hard way: seven real shows, against Claude on its own.

8.  And on taste alone, a blind judge still preferred Claude.
    We're showing you that too.
    (Say this one straight and confident. Honesty is the point.)

9.  Doors open at seven. This time, the room is ready.

10. House Mix is live. Open a show and check every receipt.

About 45 seconds of talking in total. The narrator explains the screens between your lines.

If you can't record video: voice only is fine (phone voice memo, same rules, quiet room).
I'll put your voice over the product instead of a face bubble.
