House Mix film: what you say to camera
======================================

How to record
- Laptop or phone camera, landscape (horizontal), lens at eye level.
- Light in front of you (window or lamp behind the camera), quiet room, plain wall.
- Look into the lens, not at the screen.
- Leave 2 seconds of silence before and after each line.
- Say each line twice (two takes). Energetic: stand if you can, smile on line 1,
  lean in on line 5, speed up a little through lines 8 to 10, and land the last word
  of every line clearly. Fast is fine; rushed is not.
- Say the number first, softly ("line one"), so I can find it.
- One file is fine for everything. Save it in ~/Downloads as housemix-face.mov
  (or .mp4) and tell me when it is there.

THE PROBLEM AND YOU
1.  I'm Prashant, and I built House Mix for the people who run the room.

2.  Every night they guess the music, the opener, the bar and the sponsor,
    for a crowd they've never met.

3.  So House Mix asks Qloo who's really coming.
    (Qloo is said "clue".)

4.  This is House Mix.
    (A reveal: a short pause before "House Mix".)

SPONSORS
5.  Now, the part sponsors care about.

6.  If you're the brand paying for tonight, here's how you know you belong.

EVERY KIND OF NIGHT (quick, one breath each)
7.  And it isn't only stadiums.

8.  A comedy club gets openers who fit the headliner's crowd,
    and podcasts for the interval.

9.  A festival gets every stage programmed for the crowd moving between them.

10. And a restaurant gets its whole service: the playlist, the drinks partner,
    the late lounge set.

11. Five kinds of nights. One crowd read. Every pick backed by Qloo.

PROOF, HONESTLY
12. We tested it the hard way: seven real shows, against Claude on its own.

13. And on taste alone, a blind judge still preferred Claude.
    We're showing you that too.
    (Straight and confident. Honesty is the point.)

THE CLOSE
14. Doors open at seven. This time, the room is ready.

15. House Mix is live. Open a show and check every receipt.

About 75 seconds of talking in total. The narrator explains the screens between
your lines: the live site, the sponsor flow in detail, Run the night, the 3D
"under the hood" and the numbers.

If you can't record video: voice only is fine (phone voice memo, same rules).
